Kineograph is a free, open-source app for drawing frame-by-frame animation on your phone, like an old-fashioned flipbook.

<b>Free forever</b>
No ads, no subscriptions and no watermark. Layers and frames are limited only by your device.

<b>Private</b>
No account and no analytics. Your work stays on your device; the app does not even have permission to use the internet.

<b>Made for every phone</b>
A small download that runs on inexpensive phones from Android 8 on, and works well with a finger or a stylus.

<b>What you can do</b>
• Draw with low latency using Pen, Pencil and Marker brushes, with pressure support
• Fill shapes, even when an outline has small gaps
• Move, scale and rotate parts of a drawing with the lasso
• Add, duplicate and reorder frames
• Use as many layers as you like
• See your previous frame with onion skin
• Play back at any frame rate from 1 to 60
• Export MP4 video and GIF, ready to share
